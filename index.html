PPC Platform (internal tool)

I am currently building PPC Platform, a self-hosted, internal-only web application that connects to the Google Ads and Meta Ads accounts I already manage. It pulls performance reports through the official Google Ads API, flags issues with a rule-based monitoring layer, and — for a narrowly defined set of low-risk actions only — can apply small automated changes such as pausing a non-performing keyword. Anything more consequential is queued for my manual review and approval before any change is made. The tool is not sold or offered to third parties; it is used only by me (and, in the future, potentially a small number of people working directly under my supervision) to manage the client accounts listed above.
