Privacy Policy - PPC Platform

Effective date: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PPC Platform ("the Tool"), an internal, self-hosted application built and operated by Daniel Vereb, a sole trader based in Hungary ("I", "me"), handles data.

Who I am

I am an independent PPC (pay-per-click advertising) specialist. I manage Google Ads and Meta Ads accounts for a small group of real clients under existing agency relationships. Contact: vereb.daniel.10@gmail.com.

What the Tool does

PPC Platform is an internal-only tool, not offered or sold to third parties. It connects to Google Ads accounts (via the Google Ads API, under my Manager account) and Meta Ads accounts (via the Meta Marketing API, under a Meta Business system user) that I already manage on behalf of my clients. It is used only by me, and potentially in the future by a small number of people I directly supervise.

What data is accessed

The Tool accesses advertising account data only: campaign, ad group, keyword and ad performance metrics (impressions, clicks, cost, conversions), account and campaign structure, and budget information. It does not access personal data belonging to my clients' end customers or website visitors.

How data is used

Data is used to generate performance reports, to flag issues through a rule-based monitoring layer, and to apply a narrowly defined set of low-risk automated actions (such as pausing a non-performing keyword or adding a negative keyword). Any action beyond this limited, predefined set requires my manual review and approval before it is applied. Data is never sold, shared with advertising networks beyond Google and Meta themselves, or used for any purpose other than managing the ad accounts described above.

Data storage and retention

Data pulled from the Google Ads API and Meta Marketing API is stored in a private database used only by the Tool, accessible only to me. Data is retained only as long as needed to provide reporting and monitoring for the relevant client account, and is deleted when an account is no longer managed through the Tool.

Third parties

The Tool integrates only with the Google Ads API and the Meta Marketing API to read and manage the ad accounts described above. No data is shared with any other third party.

Security

Access credentials (API tokens) are stored securely and are not shared. The Tool is not publicly accessible; it runs on infrastructure that only I control.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the Tool. The effective date above will be updated accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to vereb.daniel.10@gmail.com.
